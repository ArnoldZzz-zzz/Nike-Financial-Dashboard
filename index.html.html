Financial insights and strategic recommendations

Analyst summary

This is rule-based commentary, not a connected AI service or investment advice.

Recommended strategic actions

1. Protect profitability

Track gross margin, promotional depth, tariff costs and product mix.

2. Balance inventory and cash

Compare inventory and receivables with sell-through, buybacks and dividends.

3. Localise India decisions

Keep India sales, market share and pricing separate from global results.
